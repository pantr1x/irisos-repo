IrisOS is a fast Linux desktop built on CachyOS and KDE Plasma – with Iris, a voice assistant that sees your windows, clicks buttons, types for you and installs apps. Everything runs on your own PC, offline.

Meet Iris

Say “Iris” – she wakes up, listens and does it. No cloud, no account.

🗣️

Talks and listens

Whisper for hearing, Kokoro for her voice, echo cancellation so she never hears herself.

🖱️

Uses your apps

Sees every window and its buttons as a map, clicks them, writes into fields, moves and arranges windows.

🔒

Fully local

The AI model runs on your GPU through Ollama. Nothing you say leaves the PC.

🧩

Picks the right model

The installer measures your GPU and RAM and offers 4B, 9B, 14B or 27B – you see how each will run before you choose.

🎮

Steps aside in games

When a game starts she moves off the GPU, so nothing stutters. CachyOS kernel and gaming packages included.

🎛️

You're in control

Iris Center shows her memory use and history, lets you chat, switch off what she may do and write her personality.

Download

The ISO is split into parts because GitHub allows 2 GB per file.

Loading the latest ISO…

Join the parts and check them:

cat irisos-*.iso.part-* > irisos.iso
sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS

Install

About 15 minutes. You can keep Windows or another Linux next to it.

Write it to a USB stick

Everything on the stick is erased. Use Ventoy, Impression, Fedora Media Writer, or:

sudo dd if=irisos.iso of=/dev/sdX bs=4M conv=fsync status=progress

Boot from it

Turn off Secure Boot in the BIOS, pick the USB in the boot menu. The IrisOS installer starts by itself.

Follow the installer

Language, keyboard, disk (erase, install alongside, or your own partitions), user and the AI model for Iris. Press T any time for a terminal, L to see the log.

Log in and say “Iris”

On the first start IrisOS downloads Iris's model with a progress bar, then she introduces herself.

Updates

After you turn on your PC, IrisOS checks for updates and asks you – it tells you what's new and whether anything needs care (new kernel, drivers, Arch news). Nothing installs without your OK.

IrisOS updates come from the [irisos] pacman repository on this GitHub. It's added automatically; by hand it looks like this:

[irisos]
SigLevel = Optional TrustAll
Server = https://github.com/pantr1x/irisos-repo/releases/download/$arch

Update any time from the Welcome app (Update packages) or with sudo pacman -Syu.
